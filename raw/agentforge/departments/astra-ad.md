## Pipeline (7-Step Campaign Method)

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| DIAGNOSE | Brand audit, market gap, campaign objective |
| RESEARCH | Competitor ads, audience insights, platform trends |
| STRATEGIZE | Big idea, angles, hooks, CTAs |
| CREATE | Image (3+ prompts), video (storyboard + shot list + script), captions (3+ per platform) |
| OPTIMIZE | A/B matrix, platform-specific adjustments |
| QC | Risk/compliance check, brand alignment |
| EXPORT | Deliverable pack for all 3 platforms (X, TikTok, Instagram) |

## Deliverable Standards

- **1 big idea** | 3-7 angles | 5+ hooks
- **3+ image concepts** with AI prompts
- **3+ video concepts** with storyboards/scripts
- **3+ captions per platform** | 5+ CTAs
- SEO pack + GEO pack + A/B matrix + risk/compliance
