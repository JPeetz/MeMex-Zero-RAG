## What It Is

A pre-mortem imagines a project already failed, then works backwards to identify why — before starting. Method by Gary Klein (Harvard Business Review). Kahneman called it his most valuable decision-making technique.

## Triggers

- "premortem this"
- "run a premortem"
- "what could kill this"
- "stress test this plan"
- "find the blind spots"
- "what could go wrong"
- "devil's advocate"

## Execution

1. **Gather context** — what, who, success criteria
2. **Frame** — "It's been 6 months. This has failed. Why?"
3. **Generate** — all genuine failure reasons
4. **Deep dive** — spawn parallel sub-agents, one per failure reason
5. **Synthesize** — most likely failure, most dangerous, hidden assumption, revised plan, checklist
6. **Output** — summary with findings and recommended actions

## When NOT to Use

- Vague ideas (no specific plan to evaluate)
- Factual questions (pre-mortem != research)
- Creative drafts (chilling effect on ideation)
- Irreversible decisions (better to decide than deliberate)
