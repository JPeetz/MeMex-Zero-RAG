# Memex Wiki

Your personal knowledge base. Start by adding sources to `raw/` and asking your LLM to ingest them.

Pages are organized by type:
- `sources/` — One summary per ingested source
- `entities/` — People, orgs, tools, projects
- `concepts/` — Ideas, frameworks, patterns
- `synthesis/` — Analyses, comparisons, insights

## Getting Started

1. Drop a document into `raw/`
2. Tell your LLM: "Read SCHEMA.md. Then ingest raw/[filename]."
3. The LLM will create pages and update this index
