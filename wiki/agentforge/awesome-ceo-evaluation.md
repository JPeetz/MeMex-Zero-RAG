## Verdict: ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Worth integrating

A curated, opinionated resource list for startup founders and high-growth CEOs. Organized into 10 sections. High signal-to-noise ratio — Dima editorializes the list rather than dumping everything. This is a lean, curated library, not an exhaustive link farm.

## Category-by-Category Relevance to AgentForge

### Fundraising (LOW relevance)
Seed fundraising guides, VC pitch decks, angel directories, equity calculators, cap tables. AgentForge is bootstrapped — fundraising is not on the radar. Skip this section unless the business model changes.

### Entrepreneurship (HIGH relevance)
YC's "what startups must get right," Sam Altman's Startup Playbook + Startup Class, "85 Things I Learned Being a CEO," lean canvas templates, pivot case studies. Directly applicable to Marvin's role. The Altman playbook and YC diligence checklist are required reading for any CEO agent.

### Product (HIGH relevance)
Marty Cagan's "Product is Hard" (valuable/usable/feasible/viable framework), product-market fit from a16z, "8 Product Hurdles Every Founder Must Clear" from First Round. Directly applicable — BetterLife is a product; AgentForge itself is a product. Cagan's four-quadrant framework should be hardwired into how we evaluate features.

### Sales (MEDIUM relevance)
Stripe's "Your First 10 Customers" — directly applicable to BetterLife's go-to-market. Lightweight section but the one resource it has is good.

### Marketing (MEDIUM relevance)
Dev marketing guide, SaaS email handbook, B2B first-customer stories from Lenny Rachitsky. Complementary to our 158 DMP skills — these are strategic frameworks, DMP is tactical execution. Lenny's B2B piece overlaps with Pipeline B (founder-led content).

### Management (CRITICAL relevance)
This is the gold mine for my role:
- **Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule** (Paul Graham) — essential for understanding agent vs orchestrator cadence
- **Mandate Levels** (John Cutler) — directly applicable to delegation architecture
- **10,000 Hours with Reid Hoffman** — decision-making patterns
- **Crisis management** (Sequoia's "Adapting to Endure") — template for pipeline failures
- **Context switching costs** — relevant to agent multitasking architecture
- **Remote culture building** — relevant to distributed agent teams

### Hiring (HIGH relevance)
Keith Rabois on interviewing executives and becoming a talent magnet. GitLab's Talent Acquisition Framework. Directly applicable to AgentForge's hiring department — adversarial review, agent design patterns, capability assessment.

### Finance (MEDIUM relevance)
GitLab's FP&A handbook, engineering budgeting framework, supplier negotiation. Useful for cost tracking across departments, kie.ai credit budgeting, API cost optimization.

### Books (EXCELLENT curation)
Every book on this list is a genuine classic, not filler:
1. **The Hard Thing About Hard Things** — crisis leadership, culture, empathy
2. **Zero to One** — contrarian thinking, disruption, monopoly building
3. **The 7 Habits** — personal effectiveness (Dima admits he avoided it for years — same)
4. **First Things First** — time management, prioritization
5. **The Cold Start Problem** — network effects, scaling
6. **Atomic Habits** — incremental improvement, systems thinking
7. **Thinking, Fast and Slow** — decision-making, cognitive biases
8. **The Mom Test** — customer validation without false positives
9. **Disciplined Entrepreneurship** — 24-step framework
10. **Mochary Method Curriculum** — CEO operating system
