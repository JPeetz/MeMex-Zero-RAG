## PHASE 4 — SCRUTINY: RecipeSnap (ReciMe Clone Opportunity)

### Section 1: Market Validation
- Recipe app market: **$1.6B in 2026**, 13.4% CAGR → $2.5B by 2035
- **68% of consumers** now discover recipes primarily on social media (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) — not Google, not cookbooks
- The AI recipe extraction segment is growing faster than the broader market as social media food content explodes
- 36% of consumers already use ChatGPT/AI for recipe inspiration (MorganMyers Spring 2026)
- **The convergence moment:** Social video recipes + AI extraction + meal planning = untapped product category

### Section 2: Competitive Landscape
- 758 competitors but **34 funded** — still a fragmented market, no dominant player
- ReciMe: $2M funded, $2M ARR, $59.99/yr — good product, but…
  - **Pricing vulnerability:** Preplo at $4.99/mo undercuts ReciMe's $9.99/mo by 50%
  - **Platform weakness:** ReciMe is primarily iOS; Android experience weaker
  - **Feature gaps:** No guided cook mode, no ingredient cost estimates, no recipe adaptation
- Paprika: One-time payment ($4.99) — loyal users but no social media extraction. This is the whale you can displace.
- **The gap nobody fills:** A truly mobile-first recipe app that extracts from ALL social platforms, adapts recipes to dietary needs, estimates costs, AND helps cook step-by-step. No single app does all five.

### Section 3: Monetization Viability
- Current market pricing: $4.99-$9.99/mo range validated by multiple competitors
- **Preplo pricing ($4.99/mo) is the sweet spot** — below ReciMe, above "free"
- Annual at $39.99 (33% discount) = $3.33/mo effective
- **Target:** 5% conversion of 100k downloads = 5,000 paying users × $39.99/yr = $200k ARR at modest scale
- At viral scale (1M downloads, 5% conversion): $2M ARR
- Premium differentiators: dietary adaptation, cost estimates, guided cook mode, cooking streaks

### Section 4: Execution Feasibility
- **AI model:** Social media recipe extraction via vision LLM or structured API calls to platform transcript data
- **Core tech risk is moderate** — AI video/photo ingredient extraction is proven (ReciMe, Preplo, CookBook all do it)
- **30-day MVP sprint** fully viable for a mobile-first recipe saver + meal planner
- **Local-first AI** possible with on-device Phi-4-mini for recipe adaptation (not extraction — that needs cloud vision)
- **Cross-platform (iOS + Android)** is table stakes — the social media audience is split 50/50

### Section 5: Risks & Mitigations
- **Platform dependency:** Instagram/TikTok API changes could break extraction → Mitigation: Build web recipe import + manual entry as fallback
- **Competitor response:** ReciMe/Preplo could match pricing → Mitigation: Build feature moat (guided cook mode, dietary adaptation) that takes months to replicate
- **Chicken-and-egg:** Users need recipes to see value → Mitigation: Pre-load 50 trending recipes, let users import on first session
- **AI accuracy:** Ingredient extraction from videos can be imperfect → Mitigation: Show "confidence score" + let users edit before saving

### VERDICT: **BUILD** ✅

RecipeSnap hits all three criteria: **large growing market** ($1.6B at 13.4% CAGR), **clear competitive gap** (social-to-recipe-to-cook pipeline nobody owns end-to-end), **proven monetization** ($4.99-$9.99/mo validated).
