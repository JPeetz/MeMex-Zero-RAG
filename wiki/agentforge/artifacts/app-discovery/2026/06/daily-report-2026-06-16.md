## Phase 2 — GEO Competitive Research (Top 8)

### RemindLink
- **Primary keyword:** "bookmark reminder"
- **Competition density:** LOW — zero apps targeting this exact intersection
- **Top 3 competitors:** Readwise Reader (too complex), Matter (iOS only, no reminders), Instapaper (no reminder system)
- **Key gap:** None of the read-later/bookmarking incumbents have reminder-on-save. Reminder apps (TickTick, Todoist) don't have bookmark workflows.
- **ASO gap:** "bookmark reminder" → 0 apps in Play Store top results. Zero-competition keyword.
- **Pocket shutdown tailwind:** Millions of displaced users searching for alternatives.

### Albo
- **Primary keyword:** "save organize content"
- **Competition density:** HIGH — Raindrop.io, Matter, Instapaper, Readwise all competing
- **Key gap:** Manual organization (no AI). Competitors have AI auto-tagging.
- **Verdict:** Too crowded. Albo's manual approach is a liability.

## Phase 4 — Scrutiny Winner: RemindLink

**Full 5-section scrutiny completed.** Verdict: **BUILD** 🟢

- **Section 1 (Market):** Strong signal. Pocket shutdown tailwind. 87% of saved links never revisited. "bookmark reminder" is under-served.
- **Section 2 (Competition):** LOW density at the intersection. Read-later apps don't remind. Reminder apps don't bookmark. Blue ocean.

- **Section 4 (Mobile-Fit):** 9/10. Share Sheet integration, push reminders, widgets. Born mobile.
- **Section 5 (Execution):** Feasible. 30-day MVP by 2-person team. Clear GTM (Pocket migration SEO + cross-platform launch).
