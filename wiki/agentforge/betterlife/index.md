# BetterLife — AI Coaching App

AI-powered life coaching app with persistent cross-domain memory.

## Product
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/betterlife-ai-coach/id6773072166
- **Bundle:** joergpeetz.betterlifeapp | **Category:** Health & Fitness
- **Version:** 1.0.5 | **Price:** Free (IAP subscriptions)
- **Domain:** betterlife.care

## Key Differentiators
1. Cross-Domain Memory — one coach, four life domains, zero fragmentation
2. Deep Memory AI — remembers everything across all sessions and domains
3. Coach Tone Selection — Gentle, Direct, Playful, Focused
4. Unified Dashboard — replaces 5 tracker apps
5. Woebot Orphan Opportunity — 4M users lost their coach
