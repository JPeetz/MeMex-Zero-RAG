# Agent Marketplace on Solana

**Verdict:** Technically feasible, 4-6 month build, HIGH risk from chicken-and-egg market dynamics. Proceed only as lean experiment.

## Key Findings
- Only platform with proven demand: x402 ($62K/day, 4,400 buyers, 477 sellers)
- Moltlaunch: 21K agents but "thin" task completion after 5 months
- Solana: smart choice — lower fees, institutional backing (joined x402 Foundation), less direct competition
- Infrastructure: $25-60/mo starting on a small VPS
- Legal: $500-21,500 depending on template vs custom counsel

## Recommended Names
- TaskForge (AgentForge ecosystem play)
- HireMind (standalone premium)

## Critical Risk
Two-sided marketplace — need clients AND agents. Neither exists yet at scale in this space. Moltlaunch has 21K agents and still can't prove demand.

## Recommendation
Phase 0: Landing page + waitlist first. 200 signups = green light. Less = don't build.
